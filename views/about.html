About me

Hi!

My name is Linda Wong and I am an illustrator based in Stockholm, Sweden. Before my pursuit of becoming an artist, I worked as a biomedical scientist in the clincial physiology department of Karolinska University Hospital. During that time I realized that art can not solely be my hobby, I wanted to become a full time artist. I work mainly with watercolors but I also use markers, gouache, ink and digital programs. My art is inspired by my love for food, my cultural identity and a lot of cute stuff.

For business enquiries or commissions, please email qinmo.art@gmail.com
